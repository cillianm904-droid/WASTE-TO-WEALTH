Right now, we are collecting PET bottles.

PET bottles are the clear plastic bottles used for water and soft drinks. Instead of ending up in drains and dumpsites, they can be sorted, recycled and turned into something useful. That is where we are starting.

What we do

  • Waste Collection

    We pick up and receive used materials, starting with PET bottles, so they stay out of the environment.

  • Waste Management

    We sort and handle waste properly so that what can be reused is kept apart from what cannot.

  • Waste Recycling

    Suitable materials are processed so they can go back into use instead of being thrown away.

  • Waste Upcycling

    Some waste is better turned into new, useful items rather than broken down. We look for those chances.

How it works

  1. Collect

    Gather your used PET bottles and message us on WhatsApp.

  2. Sort

    We sort what we receive so each material goes the right way.

  3. Recycle or upcycle

    Bottles are recycled, or turned into new items where that works better.

  4. Create value

    Waste becomes something people can use, and something worth money.
